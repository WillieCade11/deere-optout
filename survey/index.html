Notes

  1. Class Period: January 10, 2018 through May 18, 2026. (Long Form Notice, Q5)
  2. Large John Deere equipment means "Deere Large Agricultural Equipment": "agricultural equipment manufactured by John Deere, which depend for their functioning, in part, on electronic control units ("ECUs") and which include large and medium tractors (all 6000, 7000, 8000, and 9000 Series models); combines; cotton pickers; cotton strippers; sugarcane harvesters; tillage, seeding (including planters), and application equipment and sprayers." (Long Form Notice, Q5)
  3. Settlement notice: Class members found in Deere's records "will receive by mail or email a notice" from the Settlement Administrator with a Notice ID and Confirmation Code. (Long Form Notice, Q9)
  4. Deere dealer: The class covers repair services bought "from John Deere or its authorized Dealers in the United States." The settlement does not define "Dealer" further. (Long Form Notice, Q5)
  5. Service means "Repair Services": "the diagnosis, maintenance, and/or repair of any Deere Large Agricultural Equipment." (Long Form Notice, Q5)
  6. ECU: "electronic control units ("ECUs")." The settlement gives no further definition. In plain terms, an ECU is an onboard computer that controls the engine or other systems. Examples of equipment not counted above may include 5 Series and smaller tractors, Gators, and mowers.
