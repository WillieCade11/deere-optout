Opting Out of the Deere Repair Settlement

Keep your right to sue Deere. Here is how.

Opt-out deadline: postmarked by December 4, 2026

Are you in the class? Yes, if you paid a Deere dealer to repair a large tractor, combine, or similar machine between Jan 10, 2018 and May 18, 2026. Notice or no notice.

Mail your opt-out letter to

Deere Repair Services Antitrust Litigation
Attn: Exclusions
P.O. Box 58220
Philadelphia, PA 19102

Mail it on or before December 4, 2026. Send certified mail and keep a copy. One letter for each person or business that paid for repairs.

Do nothing? You stay in the settlement. You give up your right to sue Deere. You get no money unless you file a claim by December 31, 2026.
